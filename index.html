How scoring works

Tap a team’s score to award High, Low, Jack, or Game to that side. Choose the bidding team and set the bid. If they miss the bid, they score negative the bid; the other team keeps the points they earned. Press “Score hand” to add the hand to the running game score.

Each game continues across as many hands as needed until a team reaches 11. The selected number of games is played in full; match totals show game wins, with no best-of cutoff.

This starter uses the common 4-point Setback scoring. Adjusted rules can be added to match your table.
